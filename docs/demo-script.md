# Demo speaking notes (target 2:30)

**Before recording:** close other windows and notifications, zoom the browser to about 125% so text is readable, and copy the risk-case report from [test-inputs.md](evidence/test-inputs.md) to the clipboard.

| Time | Show | Say (in your own words) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | ClaimLens home page | ClaimLens is for grocery-store claims managers doing a first review of a new incident report, like a slip-and-fall. Reports are messy and inconsistent, and missing details (a witness, camera footage, whether a sign was out) are easy to overlook. |
| 0:20–0:55 | Click **Unclear / conflicting** → **Review report**. Point at the orange status banner, the Key facts tags, and an **Ask:** line. | Central task: paste a report, click Review. The banner says 8 items need confirming. Each fact keeps its source sentence. The time is tagged *Conflicting: 2:15 vs. 4:00*, not stated as fact, and it links straight to the question to ask. |
| 0:55–1:35 | Paste the risk-case report → Review. Optionally show the *before* screenshot side by side. | Main revision, from peer feedback and my own risk test. Before, a report saying "no witnesses, the camera wasn't working, she refused treatment" came back as *witness mentioned, video mentioned, treatment mentioned*. A manager could believe evidence exists that doesn't. Now negatives are preserved and nothing disputed appears as a plain fact. |
| 1:35–2:05 | Open **How this review works**. | Where AI fits: the flow, sources, tags and Keep/Edit/Dismiss work for real. The "AI" is simulated with keyword rules. The planned version uses a language model. |
| 2:05–2:30 | Show the probe result or describe it. | Limitation: a report saying a man "went down hard" with "oily residue" was rejected, because the rules don't know those words. That's why a real model is my next priority, along with requiring a note when someone dismisses a conflict. |
